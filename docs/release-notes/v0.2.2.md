A fix for the installer window.

## Fixed

**The installer window.** Opening the v0.2.1 DMG with hidden files shown put two stray folders over the ZenTerm wordmark, one of which the build left behind and should never have shipped. The window now holds the app and the Applications folder, and nothing else.

## Install

ZenTerm updates itself. On v0.2.0 or later, the update card appears in the app. For a first install, open the DMG and drag ZenTerm to Applications. macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon.

## Reporting a bug

Open an [issue](https://github.com/zen-term/zen-term-releases/issues) with the version from the bottom of Settings (⌘,).
