Scroll mode reads the terminal's own cells, so its cursor and selections stay on the text you put them on.

## Changed

**Scroll mode starts on the shell's cursor.** With text selected it starts on the start of your selection and keeps it, so `y` copies it. The cursor is a block in the cursor color, two cells wide on a wide character, and the row band is gone.

**Scroll mode selections are the terminal's own.** `v` and `V` select the way a mouse drag does, so ⌘C copies what they took. A selection reaches past the top of the screen, and `y` copies all of it. `V` and `yy` take a wrapped line whole.

**`{` and `}` stop at prompts** when your shell marks them, and at blank lines when it does not. Like every motion in scroll mode, they read on into the scrollback.

**Clicking in the pane leaves scroll mode** when it clears the selection.

## Fixed

**Scroll mode keeps its place.** The cursor and a selection stay on their text while output arrives and through resizes, where they used to jump to a nearby line that looked the same.

**Moving across CJK and emoji** takes one step per character.

**Find lands on the selected match.** Pressing ⏎ in the find field and then ⌘⇧S puts the cursor on the match the field counted, even with several matches on screen.
